Common Questions

Everything you're
probably wondering.

Do I have to pay before seeing anything? +
No. We build a fully functional live demo of your system before you pay a single cent. You interact with it, test it, and only once you're satisfied does the project move forward — and only then do we invoice.
How long does it take to go live? +
Typically 5–10 days from kickoff to live deployment, depending on the tier and complexity of your stack. Tier 01 is usually live in 5–7 days. Tier 03 can take 2–3 weeks for the full web re-engineering component.
What CRMs and tools do you integrate with? +
HubSpot, Salesforce, GoHighLevel, Airtable, Calendly, Cal.com, Google Calendar, Zapier, Make (Integromat), Twilio for SMS, and most REST API-capable platforms. If you're unsure about your stack, mention it in your application and Xavier will confirm compatibility on the call.
What kind of businesses do you work with? +
We work with service-based businesses where lead response speed and qualification directly impact revenue — agencies, consultants, coaches, SaaS companies, local service businesses with high ticket offers. If you're losing leads to slow response or poor follow-up, we can likely help.
Why is intake so limited? +
Xavier works directly on every project — no outsourcing, no junior devs handling your build. This requires time and focused attention. Limiting intake to 3 clients per month is how we guarantee the engineering precision every client pays for. We'd rather do 3 things exceptionally than 10 things adequately.
What happens after the optimization window ends? +
Your system runs independently — you own it. If you want ongoing refinement, priority support, or expansion of the system, we offer retainer arrangements for existing clients. But the base system is yours, fully functional, with no recurring fees baked in by default.
